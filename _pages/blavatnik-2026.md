I was named one of the top three young scientists in the Physical Sciences & Engineering across New York, New Jersey, and Connecticut in the 2026 Blavatnik Regional Awards for Young Scientists, selected from 176 nominations spanning 35 institutions.

I was recognized as a Finalist, with a $10,000 unrestricted prize, for pioneering cosmological simulations and interpretable machine learning methods that reveal hidden information about the Universe's structure and evolution.
