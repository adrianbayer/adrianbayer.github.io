Deeply honored to be named one of the top three young researchers in Physical Sciences & Engineering across New York, New Jersey, and Connecticut by [The New York Academy of Sciences](https://www.nyas.org/){:target="_blank"} and the [Blavatnik Family Foundation](https://blavatnikfoundation.org/){:target="_blank"}!

The Blavatnik Regional Awards for Young Scientists recognize outstanding postdoctoral researchers in the Tri-State area in three categories: Chemical Sciences, Life Sciences, and Physical Sciences & Engineering. This year, nine honorees were selected from 176 nominations spanning 35 institutions. In each category one Laureate receives $30,000 and two Finalists each receive $10,000 in unrestricted funds.

The award, as a Finalist in Physical Sciences & Engineering, recognizes work on pioneering cosmological simulations and interpretable machine learning methods that reveal hidden information about the Universe's structure and evolution. This includes fast simulations of cosmic structure, multi-probe simulation suites such as [HalfDome](/projects/halfdome/), and machine learning methods that can be trusted to discover new physics rather than simply reproduce what we already know.

A huge thank you to the [Simons Foundation](https://www.simonsfoundation.org/){:target="_blank"} and the [Flatiron Institute](https://www.flatironinstitute.org/){:target="_blank"} for the nomination and for supporting me and my research along the way, and congratulations to all of this year's honorees!
